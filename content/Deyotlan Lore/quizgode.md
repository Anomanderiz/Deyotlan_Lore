# Quizgode

## Overview

This strange, midnight black fruit grows only in the deep woods of [[Quil na Sko]]. One of many unique plants that grow in the Forest of Shadows, this one is highly prized by the [[House of the Eagle]] at the volcanic city of [[Tlecalco]] to manufacture a highly sought after alchemical potion. Trade of this fruit has recently consumed the political sensibilities of both the dwarves of [[Deyotlan]] and, seemingly, the enigmatic [[The Celestial Twins]] of Quil na Skoth.

## Description

The nighblood fruit, as its name suggests, is of a spherical form and deep of colour. With midnight black skin and flesh as pale as the moon, the fruit bleeds a thick, dark, ruby juice that unsettlingly resembles blood. The juice stains all natural fibres indelibly and exudes a cloyingly sweet smell for several hours from its bruising.

The fey of Quil na Sko often use it in their wine and other potables but do not seem interested – or aware of – its potent alchemical properties that the dwarves have discovered.
