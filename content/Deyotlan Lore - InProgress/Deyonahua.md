# Climate

In the heat and humidity of the jungles of Deyonahua, visitors soon find that what clothing the body may tolerate is drapery of fine textiles. Few pieces of stitched clothing may be seen in the dwarven and fey cultures on this land, but fine textile of all sorts are produced from plants as common as the cotton puff and as exotic as the spidersilk of Quil na Sko.

Clothing is light, does not cover all the body and allows for ample flow of air so that the wearer may be comforted in the heat of the sun and the humid breath of the thick and unforgiving jungles. However, as bronze is particularly abundant in the mountains of Deyonahua, bronze thread is woven into the borders of more expensive clothing and pieces of bronze jewellery often feature on the body in complementary ways to clothing. This is a small discomfort the dwarves bear for the sake of art and fashion. The fey also incorporate bronze wire in their garb but do so as elaborate structural elements that hold taut dramatic drapes of fabric into hoods, plumes and tails.
