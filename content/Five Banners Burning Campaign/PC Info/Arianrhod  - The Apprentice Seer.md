## Appearance

Tall, though not quite as tall as the tallest humans but certainly taller than the dwarves of Deyonahua, Arianrhod is a high fey of the great Forest of Shadows.

From what little of his body can be glimpsed through the elaborate folds, pleats and drapery of his witch's robes, he has dark skin and fine-boned features. His robes are midnight black, edged in threads of gold to create an unsettling and obscuring silhouette. Gold wire, like talons or perhaps a cage, hold his hood open but obscure his face in shadow.

Through the thin material of his robes, faint points could be seen on the sides of his head where long, pointed ears push against the material from within.

What portions of his body are visible, mostly along the arms and the legs where the robe is slashed open to reveal skin, he is marked with runic tattoos.

## Personality

As a fey of springtime, Arianrhod is curious, willing to take risks to slake his curiosity but also exceptionally scared of many things. He chose to join the Circle of the Silver Wheel due to this overwhelming fear for, they work magicks that pierce the veil between the realm of thoughts and spirits and the mortal world. By surrendering his fear to the spirits beyond the veil, he finds the courage needed to explore the world and satisfy his curiosity. Because his magic is fuelled by draining fear from him to feed the ravenous, occult beasts and spirits, he can be prone to taking risks he shouldn't and is easily convinced by rash plans as well as dangerously elaborate gambits.

He is easy to talk to despite the strange and foreboding appearance of his coven's attire and uses fey glamour constantly and heavily to project an air of authority and experience to outsiders that he does not possess.

However, when he sees a glimmer of the same insecurities and fears in others, he is quick to drop the act and befriend them, hoping to find strength in camaraderie.

In combat, he is loathe to harm people and end their mortal journeys, as his coven believes in the proper passage of time and that every mortal souls must journey through its phases, just as the moon – a sacred symbol of the soul – transitions through its phases. So, as all tricksome fey, he finds ways around this by unleashing nightmare spirits upon his enemies and ravaging their minds with fell thoughts, allowing his allies to slay the weakened foes and distancing himself from their deaths.

## Background

As an apprentice seer in the Forest of Shadows, he belongs to the Circle of the Silver Wheel, a coven of witches that hold sacred the movements of the moon and its lunar phases. From a young age, he was trained in the ways of glamour; to see the unseen, twist perception and question the nature of reality.

Growing up in the shadows of the woods, beside the great stepped pyramids of the dwarves, he was always fascinated by the stories the petitioners brought to the Matron of his circle on holy days. Dwarven mothers, fathers, crones and grandfathers – all of them brought with them pieces of the society that his people considered neighbours but never truly friends.

The stories of the three great cities of the dwarves and the alchemical masters of the volcanic city of Tlecalco and the wondrous ways in which they use the fruits of his forest had always consumed many an idle night of his.

When the call came from the Sun Pyramid of Yollotlan, from the Great Speaker himself, for three seers to accompany his retinue to the Archon's coronation – well, there wasn't much convincing that was needed for him to volunteer. His people can be obtuse, apathetic and hedonistic; they preferred to forget the world beyond the Forest and live between this world and the next in blessed commune and he did not resent them their peace. But there was an ember within him that could not be quenched but through the exploration of the world beyond. He knew it and so did the Twins. When they gathered for the vernal equinox and a call for volunteers went out, only a handful from the thousands gathered stepped forth and he could feel the gaze of the Twins upon him even before he stepped forward. Moirai, Rioghnach and him – three seers of varying ages to represent the triune experience of life and death. He had been the youngest, to represent the spring of life and the waning death. His would be the task to see to the nurturing of his people's interests, the embrace of peace and the amicable welcome of all. However, in the gaze of the Twins, he also saw the free abandon of youth, the belligerent roar of spring overcoming dolorous frost, and the embrace of adventure.

Peace, growth and happiness, but also abandon, adventure and daring. This was his mission; to nurture the interests of the Forest through daring gambits. His companions will similarly embody their seasons as they saw fit and the hope, he thought, was that those who saw the clearest may win a new victory for their people.
