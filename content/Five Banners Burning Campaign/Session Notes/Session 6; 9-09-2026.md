## Attack of the Bugs

- [[Flense]] casts a spell to give people advantage.

- [[Vaelith]] tries to convince an Auridonian guard to abandon his post and get reinforcements.

  - Fail with hope, [[Vaelith]] says, "Fucking coward. You would have died on the mountain."

    - He takes position and draws his blades.

- [[Dalrick]] asks if the net gun is ready, Admiral Ternipolia says no and it won't be ready.

  - But there are big harpoon guns on the airship that [[Dalrick]] can use.

    - He gets a critical hit on his hand cross bow.

- [[Ajax]] moves into position to east, Burrower goes underground and pops up next to him.

  - Makes an attack on [[Ajax]]

- [[Flense]] crits on the massive burrower.

  - Acid blood from the burrower coats [[Ajax]] and some of the guards.

- [[Vaelith]] tries to cast blighting strike on a smaller Tel'rothi

  - [[Tel'Gir]] prevents the connection from going through to hit the creature.

- The Burrower moves closer to the airship, misses [[Flense]]

- 3 Small Tel'rothi swarm [[Flense]], he dodges 2 of them.

  - [[Flense]] uses his whip to force the burrower and the 3 small ones back.

    - The whip makes the burrower scramble on the airship
    - [[Flense]] pulls out a bit and bridle out of his bag as he attempts to RIDE THE FUCKING TEL'ROTHI BURROWER.

- [[Ajax]] hits a small tel'rothi with his battleaxe, splits it in half

- The burrower spits acid on the airship

- [[Dalrick]] shoots a harpoon at a small tel'rothi, he misses.

- The burrower does acid breath on the airship, hits everyone.

- [[Vaelith]] uses voice of Dread to restrain the burrower with memories of its slain kin.

  - The burrower is hypnotized, it doesn't seem that ANY of the Tel'rothi are attacking [[Vaelith]]

    - "It almost seems as if they reach out for command"

- [[Flense]] gets a crit to try and tie up the burrower

- [[Ajax]] move into position back up North and becomes surrounded

- [[Vaelith]] gets a crit as he reaches out with a presence roll to explore the connection that the Tel'rothi have to him.

  - I feel an outward pulse or attention, echo locate all Tel'rothi around me.

    - I feel their desire to main and kill the big red ship.
    - I feel connected to their force.

- [[Flense]] hog ties the burrower.

- [[Dalrick]] fires a harpoon from the airship as it begins to takeoff.

- [[Vaelith]] reaches out to the Tel'rothi, and tells them to attack the source of the signal. They begin to attack each other.

- The party routes the remaining Tel'Rothi

## The Aftermath

- [[Jarlo]] brings [[Arianrhod]] out from below deck and accuses them of being a spy.

  - [[Arianrhod]]'s disguise held and still looks like an Endurathi commoner.

    - [[Flense]] makes a successful argument that he was taking shelter during the attack like everyone else.
    - [[Jarlo]] sees reason in this and gives [[Arianrhod]] back to us.
    - [[Jarlo]] pulls [[Dalrick]] aside and asks him to stay with our group and watch us.

- [[Flense]] still has a live Tel'Rothi and wishes to take it back to his camp as a mount

  - [[Vaelith]] is in awe of this and views [[Flense]] as a warrior amongst the Freemen

    - [[Flense]] tries to pay a commoner to bring the hog-tied Tel'rothi into Ashkelon in a cart
    - [[Vaelith]] rolls a successful presence roll and tells the commoner to do it for free
    - Commoner agrees, but wants money if there are any damages.

- The gang is left with the banquet and a ton of unanswered questions.
